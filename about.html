About Tasty

Serving fresh food with love since day one.

Our Story

Tasty started as a small family kitchen with one simple goal, to serve fresh and honest food to everyone who walked in. Over the years we grew into a full restaurant, but we never changed the way we cook. Every dish is still made fresh, every single day, using ingredients from local farmers we have known for years.

Our Mission

We believe good food should not be complicated. That is why we keep our menu simple, our kitchen clean, and our service fast. Whether you dine in, order online, or book a table for a special day, we want you to leave happy and full.

Meet The Team

Our head chef has over 12 years of experience in Italian and Asian cuisine, and our delivery team makes sure your food reaches you hot and on time, usually within 30 minutes.
